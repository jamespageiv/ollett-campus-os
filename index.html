        Talk track · student vignette (I'll walk through the demo scroll)

        "Hey Emma, midterms are behind you. Let me know if you want me to sign you up for some tutoring in Econ. They have some open spots on Wednesday mornings."

        Someone is going to do this. I have a version of it on my phone right now. I only gave my agent my email address and it just monitors it. About three weeks in, it had figured out what was important to me. One Saturday afternoon I got a text: "James, just a reminder, the kids won't have school on 11/2 and 11/3. Tomorrow's the last day to register for the day camp at Long Elementary. Want me to reserve a spot?"

        That was one data source, and it is full of terrible data. What if it knew all my courses, my learning materials, my faculty, all my past semesters, grades and progress? What if my agent knew every opportunity to engage with my fellow students and help me connect?

        To pull that off takes trust, which is your first company value. What builds trust? Data, and time. The more you learn about someone over a longer period, the more you know how much you can trust them.

        How it works

        Headless agents. Any surface.

        The agent has no screen of its own. A text, Slack or a command center all reach the same agents and governed data.

        The data

        Own it. Partner on it. Create it.

        Follett owns

        First-party

        • Purchases and loyalty
        • Access orders and delivery
        • Digital materials activity
        • Catalog and inventory
        University shares

        Entrusted, not transferred

        • Enrollment and add/drop
        • Learning signals, not raw grades
        • Financial aid status
        • Tutoring and advising slots
        Created with consent

        New and student-owned

        • Preferences and consent passport
        • Agent memory
        • Community activity
        • Aggregate outcome studies
        Insight travels, records do not. Clean rooms, zero-copy sharing and governed feeds. Consent, roles, audit and no model training on student data.
